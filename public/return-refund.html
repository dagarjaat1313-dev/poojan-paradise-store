Return & Refund

✦ • ✦

Our return and refund guidance for damaged, incorrect or eligible products, with a simple process for customers.

Last updated: 25 August 2026

01Return Eligibility

Return eligibility depends on the product type and its condition. Where a return is permitted, the product should generally be unused and returned in its original condition and packaging, together with the relevant order details.

02Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that appears damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact customer support promptly with your order details. We may request clear photos or videos of the product and packaging to review the claim.

03How to Request a Return

  • Keep the order/invoice details available.
  • Contact customer support with the reason for the return.
  • Provide requested evidence, such as photos or videos, where applicable.
  • Do not dispose of the product or original packaging until the request is reviewed where a return may be required.

04Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed after the returned item or claim has been reviewed and accepted. The time taken for the amount to appear can depend on the original payment method and payment provider.

05Non-Returnable Situations

Some products may not be eligible for return because of their nature, hygiene considerations, consumable status, opened packaging, customer misuse or other applicable conditions. Eligibility will be assessed based on the product and circumstances of the request.

06Customer Responsibility

Please ensure the product is securely packaged if a return is approved and follow the return instructions provided by customer support. Return requests may be declined where the product does not meet the applicable return conditions.
