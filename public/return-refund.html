Return & Refund Policy

Returns

Return eligibility depends on the product type and condition. Products must generally be unused and returned in their original condition and packaging where applicable.

If an item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact customer support promptly with the order details and supporting photos/videos where requested.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed after the returned item or claim is reviewed. Refund timing can depend on the original payment method and payment provider.
