Cancellation Policy

Before Dispatch

Customers may request cancellation before an order is dispatched. Cancellation is subject to order status and payment processing.

After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible. Return or refund rules may apply instead.

Refund

For an approved cancellation, eligible prepaid amounts are refunded through the applicable payment method, subject to provider processing times.
