Cancellation Policy

✦ • ✦

Simple guidance on when an order can be cancelled and how eligible prepaid amounts are handled.

Last updated: 25 August 2026

01Cancellation Before Dispatch

Customers may request cancellation before an order is dispatched. Cancellation is subject to the current order status and payment processing stage. We will make reasonable efforts to process an eligible cancellation request.

02Cancellation After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible. In such cases, the applicable return and refund process may apply instead.

03How to Request Cancellation

  • Contact customer support as soon as possible after placing the order.
  • Provide your order number and the contact details used for the order.
  • Cancellation requests are subject to the order status at the time we receive the request.

04Refund for Approved Cancellation

For an approved cancellation of a prepaid order, eligible amounts are refunded through the applicable payment method. The time taken for the refund to appear can depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

05Orders Already Fulfilled

If an order has already been delivered, it cannot be treated as a simple cancellation. Any eligible request will be considered under the applicable Return & Refund Policy.
