Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. Payment providers may collect and process payment information under their own terms and privacy policies. Poojan Paradise does not intend to store complete card credentials on its website.

We may share necessary information with service providers involved in payment processing, delivery, website operation or customer support when required to fulfil an order or operate the store. We do not sell customer information as a product.

We take reasonable measures to protect information used for operating the store. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for orders, customer support, legal obligations and legitimate business purposes.

07Your Choices

If you have questions about your personal information or want to request an update or correction, contact Poojan Paradise using the contact details provided on the website.

08Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version published here will apply from its stated update date.
