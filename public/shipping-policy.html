Shipping & Delivery

✦ • ✦

Clear information about order processing, dispatch and doorstep delivery for your Poojan Paradise order.

Last updated: 25 August 2026

01Order Processing

Orders are normally processed after successful order confirmation and payment verification. Processing time may vary depending on product availability, order volume and operational requirements.

02Dispatch & Delivery

After processing, orders are handed over to the applicable delivery partner. Estimated delivery timelines depend on the destination, courier availability and product availability. Any delivery estimate shown at checkout is an estimate and may change.

03Delivery Address

  • Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
  • A reachable phone number should be provided for delivery coordination.
  • Customers should check their address carefully before placing the order.

04Delivery Delays

Delivery may be delayed by circumstances outside our reasonable control, including weather, public holidays, courier disruptions, incorrect address details, high order volumes or other operational issues. We will make reasonable efforts to assist with delivery-related queries.

05Receiving Your Order

Please check the package at the time of delivery where reasonably possible. If the package appears seriously damaged or tampered with, retain relevant evidence and contact customer support promptly.

06Shipping Charges

Any applicable delivery or shipping charges will be communicated during the order/checkout process. Promotional free-delivery offers, when available, may have their own eligibility conditions.
